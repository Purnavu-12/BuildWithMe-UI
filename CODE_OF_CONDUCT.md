# Community Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique work rather than people. Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted. Respect privacy and do not publish someone else's personal information.

Maintainers may remove content, request corrections, restrict participation, or ban participants for repeated or serious violations. Decisions should be proportionate and protect affected people.

Report concerns through the repository's private maintainer contact channel when available. Do not post sensitive details publicly. The maintainer must configure a private contact route before soliciting public community participation.
